How to read the badge

  • YES FDA Drug Shortages lists a matching presentation as Current (national shortage program). Your pharmacy may still have stock of some NDCs.
  • NO No Current national listing found for that lookup (or FDA marks it Resolved). A local empty shelf can still happen.
  • UNKNOWN We could not confirm from openFDA / seeds — we refuse to invent a status. Open the FDA database.
  • Never invent shortage rows. Live results cite openFDA. Seeds show the snapshot date on the face.
